As a regular Numberphile viewer and frequent card shuffler, I was enthralled by [Persi Diaconis' dive into the randomness of card shuffling](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AxJubaijQbI). I heard this "7 shuffles" fact so early in life that I've spent years annoying friends by "over shuffling" decks of cards, and annoying them even more when I explained why I was right.

I always theorized about how I might best visualize and experiment with riffle shuffles, and at long last I've sat down and realized my dream. I invite you to look at some pretty colors and shimmering heatmaps as a series of experiments guides you through what a shuffle actually does to a deck. I hope it convinces you that "not quite random" decks really do exist, that overhand shuffling really is a waste of your time, and that you should shuffle your deck of cards 7 times.
